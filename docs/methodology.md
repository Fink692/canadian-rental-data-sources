# Methodology

## Purpose

This repository helps a renter find the authoritative starting point for common pre-lease questions. It links to original sources instead of copying dynamic records or assigning a score.

## Inclusion rules

A source is included when it is:

1. Published by a Canadian federal, provincial, municipal, police, tribunal, or Crown authority.
2. Relevant to address verification, property or permit research, tenancy rules, rental-market context, or carefully bounded area-level context.
3. Publicly describable without reproducing personal records.
4. Accompanied by a coverage statement and a material limitation.

Commercial listing portals, anonymous review sites, social-media allegations, scraped resident data, and unofficial crime rankings are excluded.

## Verification process

- Open the canonical source or its official government landing page.
- Confirm the publisher, geographic coverage, and intended use.
- Record the date the link was checked.
- Describe what the source can answer without implying that absence of a record proves legality, quality, or safety.
- Prefer stable landing pages over fragile search-result or session URLs.

## Interpretation rules

- **No safety ratings.** Reported-crime data is incomplete, shaped by reporting and enforcement practices, and often spatially generalized. It must not be converted into a claim about a resident, demographic group, exact property, or future event.
- **No condition claims from permits.** A permit or inspection status is evidence of an administrative record, not proof of present habitability or construction quality.
- **No identity claims from assessment alone.** Assessment and title records can lag. A property manager or agent may be authorized to act for an owner.
- **No legal conclusions.** Tenancy pages are starting points. Rules, exceptions, facts, and deadlines matter.
- **No endorsement.** Linking to a source does not imply that its publisher endorses this repository or BlockScore.

## Maintenance

Rows use `checked_on` in ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` format. A broken link should be replaced with the current canonical source after verifying the same authority and coverage. If the replacement materially changes coverage, update both `coverage` and `limitations`.

The directory should be rechecked at least quarterly and whenever an authority changes its portal or tenancy rules.

## Privacy

Do not add names, contact details, resident information, allegation narratives, exact incident locations, personal documents, or screenshots containing private data. Corrections should point to an official public source and discuss the source—not a person or household.
