# Contributing

Corrections and additions are welcome when they improve the official-source directory.

## Good contributions

- Replace a broken link with the current page from the same public authority.
- Add an official municipal, provincial, territorial, tribunal, police, Crown, or federal source.
- Tighten a coverage statement or limitation so readers are less likely to overinterpret a record.
- Correct accessibility, spelling, CSV formatting, or documentation problems.

## Please do not submit

- Personal names, email addresses, phone numbers, tenant or owner documents, or private addresses.
- Allegations about a person, landlord, tenant, building, property manager, or neighbourhood.
- Scraped listings, resident reviews, doxxing sources, leaked data, or unofficial safety rankings.
- Promotional links, referral codes, or lead lists.
- A claim that the absence of a record proves legality, habitability, quality, or safety.

## Source proposal format

Include:

1. The canonical official URL.
2. The publishing authority.
3. Geographic coverage.
4. What question it helps answer.
5. Its most important limitation.
6. The date you checked it.

By contributing, you agree that your original contribution is available under this repository's license. Third-party source data remains governed by its publisher's terms.
